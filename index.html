Each region covers 1° × 1° (about 88 × 111 km). Download it on the phone, then in Topo Globe tap the three dots → Add region and pick the file.

Install and check

  1. Download the APK above on the phone and open it (allow installing from your browser if Android asks). Newer test builds install over older ones and keep your regions and waypoints.
  2. Download the region file(s) too.
  3. Turn on airplane mode. Keep Location on (Quick Settings).
  4. Open Topo Globe: it opens on the globe with the menu open; tap anywhere to fold it to the three dots.
  5. Three dots → Add region → pick vic_S38E145.topo. Tap the orange square on the globe to fly into 3D terrain; 10 m contours appear as you zoom in.
  6. Three dots → My location. Without phone signal the first fix can take several minutes outdoors.
  7. Drop waypoint, Measure line & profile (tap points), Plan walking route (tap a destination).
  8. Restart the phone, open Topo Globe: Three dots → Waypoints – your waypoint is still there.
