Offline region files for **Topo Globe** (Victoria, Australia). Each file covers one 1° × 1° cell (about 88 × 111 km), named by its south-west corner like the Copernicus DEM tiles, e.g. `vic_S38E145.topo` covers 38–37° S, 145–146° E (Melbourne East & Yarra Ranges).

Install: download the `.topo` file on your phone, open Topo Globe, tap the three dots → **Add region**, and pick the file. Neighbouring regions can be loaded together; contours and routes join across their edges.

Contents per region:
- Elevation: Copernicus DEM GLO-30 at full 1″ (~30 m) resolution, slope-adaptive near-lossless coding (max error 0.25 m on ground flatter than 1:6, 1 m on the steepest ground; contour lines move ≤ 3 m).
- Waterways and water bodies: Vicmap Hydro (DataVic).
- Roads, tracks, paths and walker points of interest: OpenStreetMap.
